Q1: Why might we consider using Git?

Answer: Keeping a history of changes, enabling portability between machines, and allowing collaboration in a shared codebase via commits, PRs, and more.

Q2: What field could data science be applied to? Give an Example.

Answer: Quantitative finance is the first example that comes to mind: downloading time series of different assets in CSVs, loading them in pandas, performing analysis, and importing technical indicators—including but not limited to the RSI, MACD, and PSAR—with pandas' technical analysis library would be a solid use case. Additionally, in the past couple years, I've heard of substantial shifts to machine learning in finance; importing time series as tensors in PyTorch and building models and indicators would also be an intersting exercise!

Q3: What is the difference between Merging and Rebasing?

Answer: Merge creates a new merge commit linking branches, preserving order, names, ... of original commits to each branch, resulting in a non-linear but accurate reflection of branches' divergence and reconvergence. Alternatively, rebase rewrites history, committing as new commits with new IDs, creating a clean, linear history.
